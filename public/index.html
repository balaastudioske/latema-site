ABOUT LATEMA TRAVELS

Moving more than parcels.

Latema Travels brings passenger transport and parcel delivery together across a network of Nairobi-area stages and the Nairobi–Nakuru corridor.

LATEMAMOVE
TOGETHER✦
01 / WHO WE ARE

A transport network for everyday journeys.

Latema’s shared route information connects Nairobi neighbourhoods and towns along the Nakuru corridor. Passengers can use this site to explore the stages included in the information provided.

Parcels travel through the same network.

Latema’s station teams receive and hand over parcels along company-set corridors. Check current parcel availability and service details with the team before drop-off.
